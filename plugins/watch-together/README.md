# Watch Together

Watch YouTube, Twitch or your own video together with everyone in the voice
room, always at the same moment: when someone pauses, moves forward or puts on
something else, it happens for everyone.

![Watch Together, starting page](https://raw.githubusercontent.com/Novyse/plugins/main/plugins/watch-together/store/screen-1.png)

## How it works

1. Open **Watch Together** in the voice room you are in.
2. Paste a link and confirm.
3. Press play: from that moment the room follows the video with you.

There is no host: anyone can pause, resume, move the video or put on a new one,
without asking for control.

## What you can watch

- **YouTube** videos, live streams and Shorts.
- **Twitch** channels and past broadcasts.
- **A video file** (`mp4`, `webm`, `mov` and similar) from a direct `https`
  link.

Twitch clips are not supported: paste the channel or the video instead.

## Live streams

A live stream has no position to share, so only play and pause are kept in
sync and every device stays at the live edge. A very long video works the same
way, without jumping around.

## Languages

Watch Together speaks the language of the app, English and Italian. Change it
in the settings: the activity follows on its own.

## If the video does not start

- **YouTube** may ask you to tap play the first time, and that tap is enough
  for the whole room.
- **A video file** must be a direct `https` link to the file, not a page that
  contains it. The app plays it, so it may take a moment to open.
- **Twitch** live links need the channel to be live right now; for a past
  broadcast use the video link.
